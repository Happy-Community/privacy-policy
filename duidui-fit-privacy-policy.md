Privacy Policy for DuiDui Fit – Mind & Body 365

Effective Date: 1st March 2026

App: DuiDui Fit – Mind & Body 365
Developer: Yangocean

1. Information We Collect and Store in your device

DuiDui Fit is designed to track your mind and body wellness daily. We collect the following types of data:

Data stored locally on your device:

Health info: BMI, mood tracking, activity logs

Fitness info: daily activity check-ins

All of this data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Data collected by third-party services:

App interactions and device identifiers are collected by Google AdMob to show ads and measure ad performance.

This data is collected and shared with Google, even if you do not provide any personal information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Local health & fitness data: Used only for displaying charts, tracking progress, and enabling app functionality.

AdMob data: Used for displaying ads, measuring ad performance, and targeted advertising.

We do not sell or share your local health or fitness data with any third parties.

3. User Control & Data Deletion

Your local data (BMI, mood, activity logs) is stored on your device.

Uninstalling the app will automatically delete all local data Or choose clear all data in the app.

We do not provide server-stored data, so no additional deletion requests are required.

4. Third-Party Services

DuiDui Fit uses Google AdMob to display ads. For details on AdMob’s data collection practices, see Google Privacy Policy.

5. Security

All data transmitted off-device (via AdMob) is encrypted in transit.

Local data on your device is stored in a secure format (SQLite).
