QuQu Healthy Body – Privacy & Data Deletion

Developer: MindnBody365

Data Collection

QuQu Healthy Body does not collect, store, or share personal data on external servers. All data such as weight, BMI, nutrition logs, and goals are stored locally on your device only.

How to Delete Your Data

To permanently delete all your data, simply uninstall the app from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored information.

Data Sharing

QuQu Healthy Body does not share user data with third parties.
